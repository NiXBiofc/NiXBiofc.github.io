A SIMPLE FLOW

You stay in control of the connection.

The account is connected only after you approve Google's permission prompt.

01

Start in the launcher

Select Connect Gmail from the integration's settings.

02

Review Google's consent screen

Sign in in your browser and inspect the scope requested before granting access.

03

Open a label

Choose a Gmail label from the launcher and continue in Gmail.

!

Check the requested access.

The current launcher integration requests the gmail.readonly scope. Google classifies this as a restricted scope and it can permit access to Gmail data beyond labels alone. Always read the actual Google consent screen and the current privacy policy before connecting.
