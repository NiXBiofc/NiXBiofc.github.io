TELEGRAM · DIGITAL GIFTS

Gifts Pokup Bot

Beta v0.1

A Telegram bot for coordinating the purchase and sale of digital gifts. During the beta, requests are handled with help from managers.

Open @giftspokupbot ↗Support via the bot

HOW THE BETA WORKS

How it works

The process is not fully automated yet; managers assist with certain steps.

01

Create a request

The user reviews the instructions and creates a request in the bot.

02

Manager review

A manager reviews the order details and, if needed, contacts participants through the bot’s official channel.

03

Transfer confirmation

According to the team’s description, an order is confirmed as complete after the gift transfer is checked. During beta, this process is monitored manually.

!

Before you use the bot

The project is in beta, and procedures or terms may change. The team states that the current version does not charge a commission; check the current terms in the bot before each transaction. Digital-asset transfers involve risk: verify the order and recipient, and never share passwords, sign-in codes, or account access.

This page describes the process as presented by the team. It is not an independent audit or a guarantee of results. The NiXBi website does not accept payments or conduct transactions.
