CURRENT BETA WORKFLOW

How the process is described.

Some steps remain manual; use the bot's in-app instructions as the current source of truth.

01

Create a request

Open the bot, review its guidance, and submit the requested order details.

02

Manager review

A manager reviews the details and may contact participants through the bot's stated support channel.

03

Transfer confirmation

The team describes confirming the order after the gift transfer is checked. During beta, this process is manager-assisted.

!

Read this before transacting.

The team describes the current beta as having no commission, but this has not been independently verified. Check the current terms directly in the bot before each transaction. Digital-asset transactions carry risk: independently verify order details and recipient identity. Never share passwords, one-time codes, or private account access.

This page is not an audit, escrow guarantee, or promise of transaction outcomes. The NiXBi website does not accept orders, hold funds, or execute transfers.
