WHO WE ARE

Small team.
Room to experiment.

NiXBi is an independent team associated with NiXBiofc. We build software, test small ideas, and shape useful features through iteration rather than pretending every prototype is finished.

Our projects can be at different stages: some are desktop experiments, some are community tools, and some are still on the drawing board. We try to describe their current status honestly so people know what they are opening.
